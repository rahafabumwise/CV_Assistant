Hobbies & Interest

This section is optional, but can add personality and give employers a sense of who you are beyond academics or work experience. Used well, it can enhance your resume. Used poorly, it can distract. Why include it?

It helps humanize your application, can spark memorable conversation, and is useful for students or early career applicants to round out their resume. Also, this section can demonstrate transferable skills such as teamwork, communication, work ethic, and leadership. To maximize this section, be selective.

Choose hobbies that reflect meaningful parts of who you are or desirable qualities. Keep it brief by listing three to six items. Consider avoiding controversial or polarizing interests unless relevant.
